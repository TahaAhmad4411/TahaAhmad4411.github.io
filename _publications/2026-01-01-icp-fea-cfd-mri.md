Intracranial pressure (ICP) is a key clinical marker in brain tumour patients, but it is usually measured invasively. This work builds a patient-specific pipeline that segments the tumour and ventricles from MRI, generates a 3D brain mesh, and simulates tumour growth and CSF obstruction with finite element analysis (FEA) and CSF flow (CFD) modelling.

The simulations produce physics-based synthetic training data (9 tumour-growth × CSF-obstruction scenarios per patient, ~15k mesh nodes) for a neural-network surrogate, with the goal of a non-invasive ICP estimate from routine MRI.
